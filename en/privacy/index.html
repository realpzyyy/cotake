Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026 · Applies to CoTake Camera for iOS 1.0 and later until this policy is updated

CoTake Camera (同刻相机, the “App”) is provided by an individual developer whose name is shown on the App Store page (“we”, “us”). The App lets you capture photos and videos, review them in the App and save selected items to the system photo library. The current version is free and has no App accounts, advertising or in-app purchases.

In short

  • Capture, processing and storage happen on your device. The App does not automatically send your media, location or usage records to us or to any third party.
  • The App contains no advertising, behavioral analytics or automatic crash-reporting SDK and does not track you.
  • We only receive information you choose to include when you email us.

How data is handled

The App processes camera images, video audio, thumbnails, capture settings and necessary diagnostic records on your device. We do not operate any data upload service and do not automatically receive your media, location or usage records.

Local processing still involves data. Media files and local records may contain the capture time, device model, software version, camera source, saved orientation and any capture location you choose to record. Diagnostic records may include capture settings, formats, errors, processing times, frame statistics, thermal state, memory, battery level and available storage; they are used only on your device to check files and troubleshoot problems. The App does not use advertising or cross-app identifiers and does not use this information to build user profiles.

The App also stores a first-launch marker, a count of successful captures and the App versions for which a review was requested in local preferences, to avoid repeated interruptions. These values are never sent to us. Deleting media does not reset them; deleting the App normally removes them, although system backup and restore may retain them.

Review invitations are handled by Apple's system interface, and Apple decides whether to show one. The App cannot read your star rating or review. You can also open the App Store review page from Settings; that service is provided by Apple.

Device permissions

  • Camera: Used to preview and capture photos and videos, including the single rear camera, dual rear camera and front/rear composite modes your device supports. Capture is unavailable without camera permission.
  • Microphone: Used to record sound for videos. Video capture requires microphone permission; photo capture does not.
  • Photo library (add only): When you choose to save an item, the App asks for permission to add it to the system photo library. It does not read existing library contents. Denying this permission does not prevent in-app capture, review or keeping the original files. The App stores the save result and the resource identifier returned by the system locally to avoid saving duplicates.
  • Location (optional, while using the App): The Record location switch is off by default and resets to off each time the App starts. If you turn it on and grant permission, the precise or approximate location available at that moment is attached to new media and their local capture records, and may be included in copies saved to the photo library. The App does not track your route or request location in the background. Capture still works if permission is denied, a request times out or no location is available. Turning the switch off does not remove location already stored in existing media or photo-library copies.
  • Motion and orientation: Device gravity is used to keep on-screen controls upright and to determine the saved orientation of media. Raw continuous motion data is not recorded; the derived capture orientation may be stored in the capture configuration.

You can manage these permissions in system Settings and choose in the App whether new captures include location. Revoking a permission does not automatically delete data that has already been saved.

Storage and deletion

Photos, videos and capture/diagnostic records are stored in the App's sandbox, and thumbnails in a local cache. Media and capture records have no fixed automatic deletion period; some diagnostic records keep only the latest entry, and thumbnails are gradually pruned under the cache policy.

Deleting an item in the App removes the selected local photo or video file, but not its paired item or any copy already saved to the photo library. Related capture records, location, save/deletion records, diagnostics and thumbnail cache files may remain. Deleting the App normally removes its entire sandbox; the system “Offload App” option, which keeps data, is not the same as deleting. Manage photo-library copies separately in the Photos app.

Device backup, restore and iCloud Photos are governed by your system settings and Apple's terms, and may copy or sync media and metadata to other devices or the cloud. The App has no developer-operated cloud backup, and these system services do not give us access to your files. Before sharing media through Photos, check whether it contains location or other information.

When you contact us

The App has no automatic feedback upload. The Report a Problem option in Settings only opens your mail app; it does not send anything automatically or attach media, diagnostics or review counters.

If you email cotake.camera@outlook.com, we receive your email address, your message and any screenshots, media or diagnostic information you choose to attach. We use this only to reply to you and investigate the issue you report, never for advertising or profiling. Please include only what is needed; you can keep using all capture features without sending media, identity details or location.

This mailbox is hosted by Microsoft's Outlook.com service, and messages are stored under Microsoft's terms. We delete related emails and attachments within 12 months after the issue is resolved, unless the law requires otherwise. You can ask us at any time to delete messages you have sent earlier.

Children

The App is not specifically directed to children. Because we do not collect data off your device, we do not collect children's personal information through the App.

Changes and contact

If features or data handling change, for example by adding automatic feedback or a new third-party service, we will first update this policy and the App Store privacy details and show a new effective date on this page.

For questions about this policy, or to access or delete information you have sent us, contact cotake.camera@outlook.com.
